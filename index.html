Good to know

Questions I get before every ride.

Anything missing? Ask me on WhatsApp.

Is the €150 price per person or per tuk-tuk?+

Per tuk-tuk. €150 covers the whole vehicle for the 2-hour tour, whether you are 1 or 6 people. Split six ways that is about €25 per person.

How long are the tuk-tuk tours?+

Each of the three city tours runs for about 2 hours. If you would like to combine two routes back to back, say so on WhatsApp and I will plan it.

How many people fit in the tuk-tuk?+

Up to 6 guests per tuk-tuk, and the tour is always private to your group. For larger groups, message me and we will find a solution.

How big can a group be on a day trip?+

Day trips are priced per group of up to 4 guests: €350 for a half day (4h) and €550 for a full day (8–9h). They run in a private vehicle, not the tuk-tuk.

Where do we meet, and is hotel pick-up included?+

For day trips, pick-up and drop-off at your hotel or apartment in Lisbon is included. For tuk-tuk tours, we agree a convenient meeting point in central Lisbon on WhatsApp when you book.

What happens if it rains?+

Lisbon is dry most of the year, but if the forecast turns bad, message me and we will move your tour to another time or day at no cost.

What is the cancellation policy?+

You can cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before the tour. Nothing is prepaid, so there is nothing to refund.

Are entrance tickets and meals included?+

No. Prices cover the private tour and driver-guide. Entrance tickets to monuments, palaces and museums, as well as food and drinks, are paid separately.

Can children come along?+

Yes, families are very welcome. Children count towards the seat limit (6 in the tuk-tuk, 4 on day trips). Tell me their ages when you book so I can plan the pace and stops.

How do I book, and do I pay in advance?+

Send me a WhatsApp message with the tour, date and number of guests, or use the reservation form on this page, which opens WhatsApp with your details filled in. I confirm directly. There is no prepayment and no booking platform in between.
