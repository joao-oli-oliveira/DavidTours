Good to know

Is the €150 price per person or per tuk-tuk?+

Per tuk-tuk. €150 covers the whole vehicle for the 2-hour tour, whether you are 1 or 6 people. Split six ways that is about €25 per person.

Where do we meet, and is hotel pick-up included?+

For day trips, pick-up and drop-off at your hotel or apartment in Lisbon is included. For tuk-tuk tours, we agree a convenient meeting point in central Lisbon on WhatsApp when you book.

What is included in the price?+

Me as your driver and guide for the whole tour, and hotel pick-up and drop-off in Lisbon on day trips. Entrance tickets, meals and drinks are not included.

What happens if it rains?+

Lisbon is dry most of the year, but if the forecast turns bad, message me and we will move your tour to another time or day at no cost. The tuk-tuk is open-air, so bring a light jacket in the evening and sun protection in summer.

Do I pay in advance, and can I cancel?+

No prepayment. You pay at the end of the tour, in cash or by card. Cancellation is free, and since nothing is prepaid there is nothing to refund.

Can children come along?+

Yes, families are very welcome. Children count towards the seat limit (6 in the tuk-tuk, 8 on day trips). Tell me their ages when you book so I can plan the pace.
