Good to know

Questions I get before every ride.

Is the €150 price per person or per tuk-tuk?+

Per tuk-tuk. €150 covers the whole vehicle for the 2-hour tour, whether you are 1 or 6 people. Split six ways that is about €25 per person.

Where do we meet, and is hotel pick-up included?+

For day trips, pick-up and drop-off at your hotel or apartment in Lisbon is included. For tuk-tuk tours, we agree a convenient meeting point in central Lisbon on WhatsApp when you book.

Can we combine two tuk-tuk routes?+

Yes. Two routes back to back make a 4-hour tour. Say which two on WhatsApp and I will plan it. Routes also adapt on the day to traffic, weather and what you enjoy most.

What happens if it rains?+

Lisbon is dry most of the year, but if the forecast turns bad, message me and we will move your tour to another time or day at no cost. The tuk-tuk is open-air, so bring a light jacket in the evening and sun protection in summer.

What is the cancellation policy?+

You can cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before the tour. Nothing is prepaid, so there is nothing to refund.

Can children come along?+

Yes, families are very welcome. Children count towards the seat limit (6 in the tuk-tuk, 4 on day trips). Tell me their ages when you book so I can plan the pace and stops.

How do I book, and do I pay in advance?+

Send me a WhatsApp message with the tour, date and number of guests, or use the form on this page, which opens WhatsApp with your details filled in. I confirm directly. There is no prepayment and no booking platform in between.
